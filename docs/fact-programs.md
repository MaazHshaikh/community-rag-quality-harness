# Programs: Mentorship and Office Hours

**Doc type:** Fact sheet
**Date:** 2026-09-10

FounderHub runs two support programs for paid members: the Mentorship Program and Office Hours. Both are managed by Marcus Feld, Programs Lead, at programs@founderhub.example.

## Mentorship Program

The Mentorship Program pairs a founder with an experienced operator or past founder for one cohort.

- **Length:** 12 weeks per cohort.
- **Cohorts per year:** 3 (Winter, Summer, Fall).
- **Cohort size:** up to 40 founders.
- **Sessions:** 6 one-on-one sessions of 45 minutes, held every two weeks.
- **Eligibility:** Builder and Scale members only. Explorer members cannot apply.
- **Cost:** included in membership. There is no extra fee.

**Winter 2027 cohort dates:**
- Applications open: November 2, 2026
- Application deadline: November 30, 2026
- Acceptance emails sent: December 14, 2026
- Cohort runs: January 11 to April 2, 2027

Applications ask for a one-page summary of the company, the founder's top challenge, and the kind of mentor they want. Scale members' applications are reviewed first, but acceptance is not guaranteed for any tier. A founder can join a maximum of 2 cohorts in total.

Founders who miss 2 sessions without notice are removed from the cohort and cannot apply to the next one.

## Office Hours

Office Hours are short one-on-one calls with a FounderHub expert.

- **When:** every Wednesday, 12:00–2:00 PM ET.
- **Slot length:** 20 minutes.
- **Booking limit:** 2 bookings per month for Builder members, 4 per month for Scale members.
- **Booking window:** slots open 7 days in advance and close 24 hours before the call.
- **Cancellation:** cancel at least 12 hours ahead, or the booking counts toward the monthly limit.

**Experts:**
- Fundraising: Marcus Feld
- Legal and incorporation: Elena Duarte
- Growth and marketing: Kwame Asante
- Product and engineering: Sofia Lindqvist

Office Hours are advisory only. Experts do not review legal documents line by line and do not make investment decisions.
