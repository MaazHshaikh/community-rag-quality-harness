# Membership and Pricing

**Doc type:** Fact sheet
**Date:** 2026-09-01

FounderHub is an online community for early-stage startup founders, from idea stage through seed funding. This page lists every membership tier, what it costs, and the rules that apply to billing.

## Tiers

| Tier | Monthly price | Annual price | Seats |
|---|---|---|---|
| Explorer | Free | Free | 1 |
| Builder | $29 | $290 | 1 |
| Scale | $99 | $990 | 3 |

**Explorer** members can read all public discussion threads and attend the monthly Demo Night as viewers. They cannot post new threads, book office hours, or apply to the mentorship program.

**Builder** members get full posting rights, up to 2 office hours bookings per month, and eligibility for the mentorship program.

**Scale** members get everything in Builder, up to 4 office hours bookings per month, priority review of mentorship applications, and 3 seats so cofounders can join under one account.

## Discounts

- **Student discount:** 50% off the Builder tier for founders enrolled in a degree program. Proof of enrollment is required and must be renewed every 12 months.
- **Annual billing:** paying yearly costs the same as 10 months, so 2 months are free.
- **No other discounts** are offered. Discount codes shared outside official FounderHub channels are not valid.

## Billing rules

- Members can upgrade at any time. The upgrade takes effect immediately and the price difference is prorated.
- Members can downgrade or cancel at any time. The change takes effect at the end of the current billing period.
- New paid members can request a full refund within **14 days** of their first payment. After 14 days, payments are non-refundable.
- If a payment fails, the account stays active for a 7-day grace period before it drops to the Explorer tier.
