# Code of Conduct

**Doc type:** Fact sheet
**Date:** 2026-08-15

Every FounderHub member agrees to this code of conduct when they join. It applies to discussion threads, direct messages, online events, and the in-person Summit.

## Core rules

1. **Be respectful.** No harassment, personal attacks, or discrimination based on identity, background, or company stage.
2. **No unsolicited pitches.** Do not pitch your product or ask for investment in direct messages unless the other member has asked you to.
3. **Self-promotion has one home.** Promote your own product only in the #show-and-tell channel, and no more than once per week.
4. **The Vault rule.** Metrics, revenue numbers, and fundraising terms that members share in threads are confidential. Do not repost, screenshot, or share them outside FounderHub without the author's written permission.
5. **No poaching.** Do not use FounderHub to recruit employees away from another member's company.
6. **Disclose conflicts.** If you are an investor, or you work for a vendor, say so when you give advice on related topics.

## Enforcement

FounderHub uses a three-step enforcement process:

1. **First violation:** a written warning from a moderator.
2. **Second violation:** a 30-day suspension. The member can read but cannot post, message, or attend events.
3. **Third violation:** permanent removal from FounderHub with no refund.

Serious violations, such as harassment, threats, or leaking Vault-protected information, can lead to immediate permanent removal without a warning.

## Reporting

Report a violation by emailing conduct@founderhub.example or by using the "Report" button on any post. The moderation team responds to every report within **72 hours**. Reports are confidential, and the reported member is not told who filed the report.

Members can appeal a suspension or removal once, within 14 days, by emailing appeals@founderhub.example. Appeals are reviewed by a moderator who was not involved in the original decision.
