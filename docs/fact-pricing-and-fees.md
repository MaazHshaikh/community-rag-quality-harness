# Pricing and Fees

**Doc type:** Fact sheet
**Date:** 2026-09-01

ZoisPay is a payment gateway for online merchants. It operates in six countries: the United States, the United Kingdom, Germany, the United Arab Emirates, Pakistan, and Kenya. This page lists every fee ZoisPay charges. There are no setup fees and no monthly fees.

## Payment method fees

| Payment method | Fee per successful payment | Available in |
|---|---|---|
| Cards, domestic | 2.9% + $0.30 | All 6 countries |
| Cards, international | 4.4% + $0.30 | All 6 countries |
| Mobile wallets | 2.2% + $0.20 | All 6 countries |
| Direct carrier billing | 12% flat, no fixed fee | Pakistan, Kenya, UAE |
| Bank transfers | 0.8%, capped at $5 | All 6 countries |

A card is international when it was issued in a different country from the merchant's registered country. The international rate is the domestic rate plus a 1.5% surcharge.

Fixed fees are listed in USD. For other settlement currencies, ZoisPay charges the equivalent amount at that day's mid-market rate.

**Direct carrier billing** charges the customer's mobile phone bill. Each carrier billing payment must be between $0.50 and $50. Payments outside that range are declined before they reach the carrier.

Failed and declined payments are free. ZoisPay only charges for successful payments.

## Currency conversion

Customers can pay in 38 presentment currencies. Merchants are paid out in one of 6 settlement currencies: USD, EUR, GBP, AED, PKR, and KES.

When the presentment currency differs from the merchant's settlement currency, ZoisPay converts the payment at the mid-market rate plus **1.75%**. There is no conversion fee when the two currencies match.

## Payout fees

| Payout type | Fee |
|---|---|
| Standard payout to a bank account | $0.25 per payout |
| Payout to a mobile wallet | $0.50 per payout |
| Instant payout | 1% of the amount, minimum $0.50 |

## Volume pricing

Merchants processing more than **$250,000 per month** for 3 months in a row can request custom pricing. Requests go to sales@zoispay.example and receive a reply within 5 business days.

Refund and dispute fees are listed on the Refunds and Disputes page.
