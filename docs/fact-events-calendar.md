# Events Calendar: November 2026 to March 2027

**Doc type:** Fact sheet
**Date:** 2026-10-01

All times are US Eastern Time (ET). Online events run on the FounderHub event platform. The link is emailed to registered members 1 hour before each event.

## Recurring events

**Demo Night** takes place on the first Thursday of every month, 7:00–8:30 PM ET, online. Five founders each give a 5-minute demo followed by 5 minutes of Q&A. Builder and Scale members can apply to present. Explorer members can watch but cannot present.

Upcoming Demo Nights:
- November 5, 2026
- December 3, 2026
- January 7, 2027
- February 4, 2027
- March 4, 2027

**Pitch Practice** takes place every Tuesday, 6:00–7:00 PM ET, online. Groups are capped at 8 founders. Each founder pitches for 3 minutes and receives written feedback from the group.

## Special events

**Fundraising Workshop: Pre-Seed Basics** on November 19, 2026, 12:00–2:00 PM ET, online. Hosted by Marcus Feld, FounderHub Programs Lead. Free for Builder and Scale members.

**Year-End Founder Retro** on December 16, 2026, 5:00–6:30 PM ET, online. An open session where members share what worked and what failed in 2026.

**FounderHub Summit 2027** on March 12–13, 2027, in person in Lisbon, Portugal. Capacity is 300 attendees.
- Ticket price for Builder and Scale members: $149
- Ticket price for Explorer members and non-members: $349
- Early-bird tickets at $99 for paid members are available until January 15, 2027.

## Registration rules

- Registration for every event closes **48 hours** before the start time.
- Members who register but do not attend 3 events in a row lose the ability to register for 30 days.
- Demo Night and the Summit main stage are recorded. Recordings are posted for paid members within 5 days. Pitch Practice sessions are never recorded.
- Summit tickets are refundable until February 12, 2027 (one month before the event). After that, tickets can be transferred to another member but not refunded.

Questions about events go to events@founderhub.example.
